📦 Black Box Recording

Comprehensive Accident Telemetry

NovaShield continuous recording captures every critical vehicle parameter before, during, and after an incident.

🕐

Date & Timestamp

Millisecond-accurate synchronization via GPS atomic time servers for legal timestamping.

📍

GPS Coordinates

Real-time latitude, longitude, altitude, and heading logging with ±2.1m precision.

🏎️

Vehicle Speed

Continuous speed calculation logged at 10Hz to measure pre-impact velocity deceleration.

📐

3-Axis Acceleration

X, Y, and Z axis G-force logging up to ±16G to capture exact shock impact direction.

🔄

Gyroscope & Lean Angle

Measures bike tilt, pitch, and roll rate up to ±2000°/s to detect slide and rollover events.

💥

Impact Peak Force

Calculates peak impact vector to assist trauma doctors in predicting internal injuries.

🤖

AI Confidence Score

On-device neural filter score confirming collision vs normal pothole or sudden braking.

🧑

Driver Behaviour

Logs aggressive acceleration, hard cornering, and braking patterns prior to crash.

🎥

Loop Video Footage

Encrypted HD camera video clip automatically locked during crash detection.

🔔

Emergency Alert Log

Record of SMS delivery, phone calls dispatched, and response ACK from emergency services.

🔋

Battery & System Health

Continuous monitoring of internal 3.7V LiPo reserve power and waterproof seal pressure.

☁️

Cloud Sync State

Real-time encrypted Firebase Firestore & RTDB backup status for instant remote retrieval.

📄

Official Crash Investigation Report

NovaShield generates standardized PDF forensic reports recognized by police departments, medical responders, and insurance adjusters for fast claim processing.
