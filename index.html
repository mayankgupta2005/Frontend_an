✨ Features

Everything You Need for Rider Safety

A complete suite of safety features packed into one smart device.

💥

Automatic Crash Detection

IMU sensors with AI algorithms detect crashes instantly and trigger the emergency response pipeline.

📍

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Live GPS tracking with ±2m accuracy. Family members can monitor ride progress in real time.

🆘

Emergency SOS

Automatic SOS alerts sent to emergency contacts, police, and nearby hospitals within 30 seconds.

🤖

AI Crash Verification

Machine learning algorithms verify crashes to reduce false positives and ensure accurate alerts.

📦

Black Box Data Recording

Records speed, acceleration, gyroscope, GPS, and impact data — like an aircraft black box.

🎥

Video Recording

Continuous video recording with loop storage. Crash footage is preserved automatically.

☁️

Cloud Backup

All data is backed up to the cloud in real time. Access accident reports from anywhere.

📊

Ride Analytics

Track riding patterns, driving score, and receive insights to improve road safety habits.

🔋

Battery Backup

Built-in battery ensures the device keeps working even after the vehicle's power is cut in a crash.

🔧

Universal Bike Installation

Compact, waterproof design fits any motorcycle. Simple installation with no modifications needed.
